Model background and Oct 22 historical results

MODEL / RERANKER

Qwen3-Reranker-4B

A larger multilingual reranker for workflows that can tolerate more latency to improve candidate ordering.

93.67%Mean Top-1 across five embedders
6.29 sMean time to rerank 50 documents
3.986 GiBTested Q8_0 weights

When is it worth considering?

The highest overall Top-1 in this run. Compared with the 0.6B reranker, the gain is larger for Chinese-to-English than English-to-English retrieval.

Reranking 50 documents takes several seconds on average on the tested Apple M3 Ultra. This does not guarantee performance on other hardware, and relevant documents outside the candidate set still cannot be recovered.
