MODEL / EMBEDDER

Qwen3-Embedding-8B

The largest Qwen embedder in this benchmark. It represents text with 4,096-dimensional vectors and is worth evaluating when standalone retrieval quality matters.

85.67%Mean standalone Top-1
51.48 msMean query + search time
7.494 GiBTested Q8_0 weights

When is it worth considering?

The highest average standalone Top-1 across the three directions, with strong Chinese-to-Chinese and Chinese-to-English results.

A larger model does not win every direction: it trails 4B by one task on English-to-English retrieval here. Weigh reranking gains against latency and weight size.
