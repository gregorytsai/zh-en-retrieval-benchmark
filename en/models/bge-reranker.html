Model background and Oct 22 historical results

MODEL / RERANKER

BGE-reranker-v2-m3

A multilingual model that scores each query–passage pair after initial vector retrieval and reorders the candidates by relevance.

87.47%Mean Top-1 across five embedders
0.88 sMean time to rerank 50 documents
0.592 GiBTested Q8_0 weights

When is it worth considering?

The fastest of the three rerankers on average in this test. The official model card also positions it as an accessible multilingual option.

It can only reorder retrieved candidates, not recover answers outside the candidate set. Its score is not the probability that an answer is correct.
