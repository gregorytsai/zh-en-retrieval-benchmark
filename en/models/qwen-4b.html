MODEL / EMBEDDER

Qwen3-Embedding-4B

A mid-sized multilingual Qwen embedder with larger model and vector representations. It can search on its own or feed a reranker.

82.33%Mean standalone Top-1
40.65 msMean query + search time
3.986 GiBTested Q8_0 weights

When is it worth considering?

Higher standalone Top-1 than the 0.6B embedder in this run. With the same Qwen 4B reranker, its average Top-1 across directions matches the 8B embedder.

Equal averages do not imply identical per-query results or behavior on every corpus. The test corpora are small, so the same gaps should not be assumed for large knowledge bases.
