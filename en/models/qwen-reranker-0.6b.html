Model background and Oct 22 historical results

MODEL / RERANKER

Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B

The smaller multilingual Qwen reranker evaluates which retrieved candidates are more relevant to the query.

89.20%Mean Top-1 across five embedders
1.16 sMean time to rerank 50 documents
0.595 GiBTested Q8_0 weights

When is it worth considering?

Higher overall Top-1 than BGE reranking in this run, with lower latency than the 4B reranker.

It does not always beat BGE on cross-language retrieval. Correct templates and yes/no scoring are required. Each request reranks 50 documents here; changing the candidate count affects quality and time.
