WHAT THE NEW RUN ADDS

Three observations worth noticing

A larger reranker helps different metrics differently.

The 4B reranker has a higher nDCG estimate than 0.6B in all six conditions. Yet legal-clause Top-1 falls from 19.30% to 17.54%. Better top-ten ranking does not guarantee a better first result.

Reranking may offer no clear gain for Chinese search.

On the MIRACL Chinese subset, 0.6B reranking changes Top-1 from 70% to 69%, while nDCG rises from 0.832 to 0.839. Both paired intervals cross zero; validate on your own queries.

Higher ranking quality costs several seconds.

Median reranking time is about 5.4–5.9× that of 0.6B. Across six equally weighted conditions, Top-1 improves by 2.64 pp and nDCG by 0.021. The tradeoff depends on your latency budget.

These are local observations, not claims of novelty. See methods for confidence intervals and comparison with published evaluations. See research comparison →
