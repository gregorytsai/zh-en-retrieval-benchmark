METHODS / REPRODUCIBILITY

How the test works, and what it can establish.

Compare 28 setups on the same five conditions, with legal clauses reported separately. Original results, corrections and code remain available.

How is the homepage mean calculated?

Average every query within each condition, then give the three MLQA directions and two MIRACL languages weight 1/5 each. MLQA contributes 60% and MIRACL 40%; this is not family weighting or production traffic. All 28 setups cover all five tasks. ACORD is excluded while corrected reranking is pending. Cards use two decimals on a fixed 0–1 scale; tables keep four decimals. nDCG is ranking quality, not answer accuracy.

What does consistent testing mean?

Each condition fixes queries, corpus, judgments and model versions. An embedder supplies the same ordered Top50 candidates to all three rerankers. Different embedders may retrieve different documents. Reranking cannot recover documents outside that pool. The 73,164 original measurements repeat 2,613 dataset-qualified queries across 28 setups; they are not independent samples.

Data scope and bilingual direction

MLQA validation includes Chinese → English (504), Chinese → Chinese (504), and English → English (1,148), including earlier queries. The source key eng-zho means English context / Chinese question: the UI correctly labels it Chinese → English, not the reverse. MIRACL uses 200 dev questions per language and the union of all dev-judged passages: 3,786 Chinese and 7,921 English, not the full million-scale corpus. ACORD has 57 test questions and 3,931 clauses. MLQA/MIRACL labels are binary; see the legal page for corrected ACORD definitions.

Is first place a reliable winner?

These are point estimates on fixed data, without global 28-setup bootstrap intervals or multiplicity correction. The top two five-task means differ by about 0.001. Near-saturated MIRACL candidate Recall@50 limits embedder discrimination in this subset; it does not show that embedders are unimportant in large corpora. ACORD has only 57 questions and does not establish general legal capability.

What does the latency include?

Per-query total_ms = embedding_ms + retrieval_ms + rerank_ms; the final term is zero without reranking. All 73,164 rows exactly match this sum. Selecting a reranker includes query embedding, exact vector search and that reranker, not reranking alone. Mean and percentiles are computed after summing per query, not by adding stage medians. Aggregate time equally weights task means; p50/p95 are shown only for single tasks. Corrected ACORD has no new performance measurement, so its timing is blank.

Hardware and timing limits

Apple M3 Ultra, 256 GiB unified memory, Q8_0, full Metal offload and one parallel slot. Models run in separate batches, not concurrent serving. Timings exclude cold loading, indexing, network, queues and generated answers. Reranked rows reuse their embedder’s measured embedding/search time. GiB is weight-file size, not measured RAM/VRAM or a minimum requirement. Laptops and other GPUs were not benchmarked.

Published findings and this benchmark’s contribution

Qwen reports higher 4B than 0.6B reranking scores on MTEB-R, CMTEB-R and MMTEB-R; the five-task mean for each local embedder shows the same direction. Qwen uses Top100 from Qwen 0.6B; this study uses each embedder’s Top50, preventing direct absolute-score comparison. The contribution is a full local Q8_0 28-setup matrix, Chinese-to-English alongside same-language retrieval, and measured size/latency tradeoffs. Cross-language Top-1 is 86.31% for Qwen 0.6B + 0.6B versus 81.94% for 8B alone. The reverse direction was not tested; no first-discovery claim is made.

ACORD correction and original evidence

Earlier arithmetic checks passed but missed ACORD’s rule to ignore unjudged pairs. Original vectors now reconstruct full rankings, ignoring unjudged pairs and using linear gain for seven standalone embedders. All 399 original Top50 rankings match. Rerankers need new candidate scoring. Old six-task averages and ACORD scores remain historical evidence, not current recommendations. Raw result files are preserved; corrections are separate.
