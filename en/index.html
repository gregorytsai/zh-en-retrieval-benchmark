WHAT THE FULL MATRIX REVEALS

What does the complete matrix reveal?

A small embedder can approach the leading score.

Qwen 0.6B + 4B reranking reaches 0.7893 macro nDCG, 0.0033 below Qwen 4B + 4B at 0.7926. Weights fall from 7.972 to 4.581 GiB, about 42.5% less. The 4B reranker still brings a substantial latency cost.

8B does not lead in every configuration.

Qwen 8B leads standalone nDCG and, with 4B reranking, all three MLQA conditions. Qwen 4B + 4B has a slightly higher six-task mean, by just 0.0009. This is not evidence of a statistically significant win.

Larger rerankers score higher and take longer.

For each of the seven embedders, Qwen 4B reranking gives the highest six-task mean nDCG. Pooled median reranking time is about 6.22 s, versus 1.13 s for 0.6B and 0.85 s for BGE. These are local single-run stage measurements, not production concurrency latency.

These are new comparisons within this fixed corpus, not claims of first discovery. No global confidence intervals or multiplicity adjustment were computed for the 28 setups. Compare with published evaluations →
